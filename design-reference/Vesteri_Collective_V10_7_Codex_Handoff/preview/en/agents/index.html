CORE MEMBERSHIP PRINCIPLES
Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered clearly.

Membership, costs, commissions and working with other agents, explained clearly.

Why join Vesteri Collective?

Because working together can help you achieve more. Collective gives you clear ways to collaborate with other agents, offer clients a wider choice of properties and reach buyers beyond your current contacts. The Vesteri system helps save time, keep conversations in one place and answer client questions with AI tools, LIVE viewings and property promotion. It also strengthens your pitch to sellers: even when an owner appoints one agent exclusively, other Collective members may introduce interested buyers under agreed collaboration rules. One dedicated agent does not have to mean the reach of just one agency.

Who can apply to Vesteri Collective?

Independent real estate professionals and agencies may apply. Vesteri reviews identity, professional activity, applicable registrations or licences and acceptance of its Fair Play & Trust principles. Membership is selective and applications are not automatically approved.

Can I publish property listings before approval?

No. Under the selective-membership model, the public can browse the marketplace, but publication and Collective collaboration are reserved for approved professional members. Application and verification precede access to these functions.

Does it cost anything to apply or publish listings?

Applying to Collective and publishing standard listings are free for approved members. Core Vesteri system features are free as well. You can pay for additional tools as you use them or choose a subscription. Current limits, fees and premium features are shown in your Vesteri agent account.

Do I keep my brand, clients and commission?

Yes. Vesteri Collective is built for independent professionals. You keep your brand and your direct client relationships. Your commission on a sale of your own listing remains yours unless you separately agree to share it under clear referral or co-brokerage terms.

Can I offer international properties outside my own portfolio?

Yes. Collective helps you serve buyers beyond your own portfolio. Vesteri makes selected international developments available to members, and the network is committed to cooperation and commission sharing under agreed rules. For properties in Vesteri’s portfolio, we help arrange LIVE tours, webinars and conversations with developer representatives. We also support the subsequent cross-border purchase process. The agent who brings the buyer receives the larger share of the commission. Exact terms depend on the listing and applicable law.

How do referrals and shared commissions work?

Being open to collaboration is a core Collective principle. By joining, members accept the standard rules for referrals, buyer introductions and joint brokerage, including the baseline split of responsibilities and commissions. Exceptions should be rare and require agreement from both parties. Network-wide automated deal attribution and commission settlement are still being developed and are not yet available.

How does Vesteri help with marketing and lead generation?

The Vesteri system provides LIVE property presentations, AI tools and advertising automation to help promote listings and engage buyers. Results depend on factors such as budget, targeting, demand and execution. Vesteri also commits at least 10% of its revenue to promoting Vesteri Collective as a whole. This does not guarantee any individual agent a specific amount of traffic, enquiries or sales.

Are private member channels available?

Not yet. Private discussions, local peer groups and structured buyer requests are part of the longer-term product roadmap, not current membership features. Today the focus is on property listings, clear cooperation terms and Vesteri sales tools.

What are the Fair Play & Trust principles?

Approved members are expected to provide accurate listings, respect buyer privacy and existing client relationships, follow agreed cooperation terms and act honestly toward fellow members. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to suspension or removal through a documented review and appeals process.

Do I need an exclusive agreement with Vesteri?

No. Collective is designed for independent professionals, not as a conventional franchise. Joining does not itself require exclusivity. Any specific listing rights or other contractual obligations must be separately agreed and comply with local law.

How does verification work and how long does it take?

Vesteri intends to review identity, business activity, market-specific professional credentials and agreement to the Collective standards. The time required depends on the jurisdiction and completeness of the application. No approval timeline is guaranteed.

These answers describe the proposed Collective model. Membership approval, permitted activity, fees, collaboration rights and procedures depend on the applicable published terms and local regulations.

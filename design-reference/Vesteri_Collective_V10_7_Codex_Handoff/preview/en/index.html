Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered clearly.

Clear answers about listings, agents and buying property.

What is Vesteri Collective?

Vesteri Collective is a selective network of vetted, independent real estate professionals. Its public marketplace brings together local and international property listings published by network members. You can freely browse properties, sign up for LIVE viewings and webinars, and use AI tools to learn as much as possible about a property without leaving home.

How is Vesteri Marketplace different from other property portals?

Traditional property portals mainly collect listings from agents and agencies working independently. Vesteri Marketplace is a shared sales space for a network of vetted professionals who commit to fair conduct, transparency and cooperation on agreed terms. This means your agent can help you find the right property beyond their own listings. Rather than competing only to win buyer enquiries, members are expected to work together with buyers’ needs in mind.

Can I search for local property as well as property abroad?

Yes. Vesteri is intended for local and international property searches, subject to listings actually available in each market. Explore the marketplace or contact a participating agent for guidance.

Will one agent help me find properties across the network?

Yes, if an agent agrees to guide your search. They can learn what you need, review listings from other Collective members and contact agents with suitable properties. The agents may also arrange a joint LIVE viewing or webinar and help guide you through the buying process. Communication, presentations and collaboration can take place within the Vesteri system. The scope of support depends on what you agree with your agent.

Can I buy directly through Vesteri or a Collective member?

Property transactions are handled through the relevant listing professional and other transaction parties. Vesteri Marketplace facilitates discovery and contact, and Vesteri may also directly represent selected international developments. Transaction arrangements vary by property and country.

Are all properties legally verified by Vesteri?

No. We review Collective members, their professional activities and their commitment to shared standards. This does not mean that every property has automatically undergone legal or technical due diligence. Selected overseas properties offered directly by Vesteri may undergo a different review process, but buyers should still confirm exactly what has been checked. We plan to expand buyer support, such as helping find an independent lawyer for property due diligence or providing guidance through a purchase. These services are not yet guaranteed for every transaction. Before buying, always consider independent legal and technical checks of the property.

How do I buy property abroad safely?

Check ownership, legal documentation, applicable taxes, financing rules, the developer’s background and the total cost of purchase. Use qualified, independent legal advisers in the relevant jurisdiction. Vesteri Marketplace does not replace legal due diligence, but we can support your process, including helping you find independent legal advisers and connecting you with people in our network who can assist. The assistance available depends on the property and destination.

Can I join a LIVE property viewing?

Where an agent schedules an Vesteri LIVE property presentation, buyers may view the property and ask questions remotely. LIVE availability depends on each listing and agent.

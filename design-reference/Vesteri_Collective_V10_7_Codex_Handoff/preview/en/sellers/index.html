WHAT ABOUT EXCLUSIVITY AND FEES?

Agree on the sales plan before the terms.

An exclusive agreement can give one agent clear responsibility while still allowing collaboration across Collective. The commission rate alone does not determine the quality of service or the advertising budget. Ask what is included in the fee, who pays for additional promotion, when and how often the agent plans to hold LIVE property viewings, and how frequently you will receive progress reports.

  • One agent accountable for communication and managing the sale
  • Agreed rules for sharing the listing across the network
  • Transparent fees and a clear promotional plan
COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you list your property.

Straight answers about agents, exclusivity, fees and collaboration.

Do I have to sign an exclusive agreement?

There is no single mandatory arrangement for every property. You agree the type, length and scope of your contract with your chosen agent. If you opt for exclusivity, check that the agreement allows collaboration with other Collective members.

Will every Collective agent sell my property?

Your listing can be made available to other eligible members, subject to your agreement with your agent and the applicable collaboration rules. That does not mean every agent will actively promote it, and a sale is not guaranteed.

Who will contact me and conduct viewings?

Your appointed agent remains your primary point of contact. If another member helps with the sale, the roles and viewing arrangements should be agreed in advance.

Can I choose my own agent?

Yes. Choose a Collective member yourself, or contact Vesteri to discuss how to find the right person to manage your sale.

How much does selling through Collective cost?

Fees, services and any additional advertising budget are agreed before you sign a contract. There is no single published fee for every property. Ask your agent for a clear proposal and sales plan.

What if my agent does not follow the agreed plan?

Before listing, agree on activities, progress updates and how the relationship may end. Collective members commit to network standards, but your contractual rights and obligations follow your agreement with the agent.

Does Vesteri check the legal status of each property?

Approving professional members does not automatically mean verifying the legal or technical condition of every property. Agree which documents and independent checks are needed for your transaction.

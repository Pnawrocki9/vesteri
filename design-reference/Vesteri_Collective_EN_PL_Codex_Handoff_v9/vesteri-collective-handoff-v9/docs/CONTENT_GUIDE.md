# Content and localization guardrails

## Core positioning

Vesteri Collective is a selective professional network of approved independent agents. Buyers browse the marketplace without joining. Agents maintain independence, own client relationships and negotiate cooperation/commission terms among themselves. Local and international properties receive equal respect. Estalara powers existing marketplace tools but phase 1 does not change them.

## Approved copy priorities

- **EN:** “Independent agents. Stronger together.” / “Your agent. The whole network.” / “Stay independent. Belong to something greater.”
- **PL:** “Niezależni agenci. Razem możemy więcej.” / “Twój agent. Możliwości całej sieci.” / “Zachowaj niezależność. Zyskaj siłę współpracy.”
- Buyer description EN: “Explore local and international property listings, discover experienced real estate professionals, and get personalized assistance in finding the property that best matches your needs.”
- Shared standards EN: “One Network. Shared Standards.” No comparison implying Vesteri brokerage listings outrank Collective members' listings.
- Keep Fair Play & Trust. No Community homepage section. Any mentions of private discussion features or automated commission attribution must transparently describe them as not yet launched.
- Never imply buyer must join the professional network. Don't promise lead volume or verified property title due diligence from agent approval alone.
- The full source of truth for final wording is BOTH approved HTML mockups, including 19 FAQs per language. No AI paraphrasing without owner approval. Keep Polish natural rather than English-syntax calques.
- Claims about dedicating at least 10% of revenue to promotion are subject to final binding commercial wording, including revenue base and timeframe. Flag anything legally ambiguous.

## SEO and languages

Use existing production locale routing. Correct `<html lang>`, locale-specific title/meta description, canonical and reciprocal `hreflang`. Avoid duplicate or English-language structured FAQ on the Polish page. Do not remove/break other languages presently served by Vesteri.com. Keep alt text translated and readable.

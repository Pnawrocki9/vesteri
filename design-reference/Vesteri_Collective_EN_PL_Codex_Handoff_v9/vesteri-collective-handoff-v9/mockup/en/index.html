Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered clearly.

Explore how the international property marketplace works, what it means to join Vesteri Collective and how our independent professionals collaborate.

What is Vesteri Collective?

Vesteri Collective is a selective network of verified independent real estate professionals. Its public marketplace brings together local and international property listings represented by participating members. Buyers can browse properties without applying for professional membership.

How is Vesteri Marketplace different from other property portals?

Traditional property portals primarily aggregate listings. Vesteri Marketplace is designed as the shared sales space of a verified professional network whose members commit to fair play, transparent cooperation and being open to agreed referral or co-brokerage arrangements. Our ambition is to help buyers find suitable properties beyond one agent’s inventory, rather than competing solely for leads. Availability and cooperation terms vary by listing; verification does not guarantee any particular transaction outcome.

Can I search for local property as well as property abroad?

Yes. Vesteri is intended for local and international property searches, subject to listings actually available in each market. Explore the marketplace or contact a participating agent for guidance.

Will one agent help me find properties across the network?

Where an agent agrees to act as your main point of contact, they can help assess your needs and explore eligible listings offered by other members. The precise scope of representation, introductions and local expertise depends on the participating professionals and applicable agreements.

Can I buy directly through Vesteri or a Collective member?

Property transactions are handled through the relevant listing professional and other transaction parties. Vesteri Marketplace facilitates discovery and contact, and Vesteri may also directly represent selected international developments. Transaction arrangements vary by property and country.

Are all properties legally verified by Vesteri?

No. Collective member verification screens professional credentials and conduct, not every property’s legal title or condition. Listing information must be checked with the responsible agent, and buyers should undertake independent legal and technical due diligence.

How do I buy property abroad safely?

Check ownership, legal documentation, applicable taxes, financing rules, the developer’s background and the total cost of purchase. Use qualified, independent legal advisers in the relevant jurisdiction. Vesteri Marketplace does not replace legal due diligence, but we can support your process, including helping you find independent legal advisers and connecting you with people in our network who can assist. The assistance available depends on the property and destination.

Can I join a LIVE property viewing?

Where an agent schedules an Estalara LIVE property presentation, buyers may view the property and ask questions remotely. LIVE availability depends on each listing and agent.

Who can apply to Vesteri Collective?

Independent real estate professionals and agencies may apply. Vesteri reviews identity, professional activity, applicable registrations or licences and acceptance of its Fair Play & Trust principles. Membership is selective and applications are not automatically approved.

Can I publish property listings before approval?

No. Under the selective-membership model, the public can browse the marketplace, but publication and Collective collaboration are reserved for approved professional members. Application and verification precede access to these functions.

Does it cost anything to apply or publish listings?

The proposed model offers free applications and free standard listing publication for approved members, with optional paid Estalara tools. Any limits, fees or paid features must be clearly set out in current published membership terms.

Do I keep my brand, clients and commission?

Yes. Vesteri Collective is built for independent professionals. You keep your brand and your direct client relationships. Your commission on a sale of your own listing remains yours unless you separately agree to share it under clear referral or co-brokerage terms.

Can I offer international properties outside my own portfolio?

Yes. One of the advantages of Collective is the opportunity to help your clients beyond your own listings. Vesteri intends to make selected overseas developments available to participating members, while members are expected to welcome transparent cooperation and agreed commission sharing on eligible listings. Rights to market each property, and the terms of collaboration, depend on owner authorisation, contracts and local law.

How do referrals and shared commissions work?

Openness to cooperation is a core Collective principle. Members should agree referral, buyer-introduction or co-brokerage arrangements in advance, including responsibilities and commission splits. Exact terms depend on the listing, the parties and local law. A platform-wide automated attribution and commission-settlement system is still under development, so it must not be assumed to be available today.

How does Estalara help with marketing and lead generation?

Estalara provides LIVE property presentations, AI-powered assistance and automated advertising workflows to help agents market listings and engage potential buyers. Results depend on budgets, targeting, property demand and execution. Vesteri also commits to dedicate at least 10% of its revenues to promoting Vesteri Collective as a whole. This shared-promotion commitment does not guarantee individual traffic, leads or sales; the revenue base and reporting approach must be defined in the formal terms.

Are private member channels available?

Not yet. Private discussions, local peer groups and structured buyer requests are part of the longer-term product roadmap, not current membership features. Today the focus is on property listings, clear cooperation terms and Estalara sales tools.

What are the Fair Play & Trust principles?

Approved members are expected to provide accurate listings, respect buyer privacy and existing client relationships, follow agreed cooperation terms and act honestly toward fellow members. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to suspension or removal through a documented review and appeals process.

Do I need an exclusive agreement with Vesteri?

No. Collective is designed for independent professionals, not as a conventional franchise. Joining does not itself require exclusivity. Any specific listing rights or other contractual obligations must be separately agreed and comply with local law.

How does verification work and how long does it take?

Vesteri intends to review identity, business activity, market-specific professional credentials and agreement to the Collective standards. The time required depends on the jurisdiction and completeness of the application. No approval timeline is guaranteed.

These answers describe the proposed Collective model. Membership approval, permitted activity, fees, collaboration rights and procedures depend on the applicable published terms and local regulations.

# GDPR — YOUR RIGHTS

**How to exercise your personal data rights at Vesteri** · Version 1.0

## You have the following rights:

- **Access** (Art. 15) — confirmation of processing, a copy of your data, information about purposes, recipients, periods, and the logic of profiling;
- **Rectification** (Art. 16) — correction of inaccurate data, completion of incomplete data;
- **Erasure** (Art. 17) — where the data is no longer necessary, consent has been withdrawn, an effective objection has been raised, or the processing was unlawful;
- **Restriction** (Art. 18) — for the time of verifying accuracy or an objection, or instead of erasure;
- **Portability** (Art. 20) — receiving the data you provided to us on the basis of consent or contract in a structured format (e.g. JSON/CSV) and transmitting it to another controller;
- **Objection** (Art. 21) — to processing based on legitimate interest; an objection to direct marketing is always effective;
- **Withdrawal of consent** (Art. 7(3)) — at any time, without affecting prior processing; easiest via "Manage privacy" in the footer/your account;
- **Rights concerning profiling** (Art. 22) — human review of your score, expressing your point of view, contesting the score;
- **Complaint** — to the President of UODO: ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland · uodo.gov.pl. You may also complain to the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

## How to submit a request

Write to: **privacy@vesteri.com** (subject: "GDPR") or by post (address in the Privacy Policy). Describe which right you are exercising. We may ask for additional identity verification — proportionate, and only where necessary.

**Deadlines:** we respond without undue delay, at the latest within **one month**; for complex requests we may extend by a further 2 months, informing you of the extension. Exercising your rights is free of charge (for manifestly excessive requests we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse — with justification).

**Data handed to a developer:** the developer is an independent controller — at your request we will forward your request to them, and the data sharing agreement obliges them to honour your rights.

*In case of discrepancies, for consumers habitually resident in Poland the Polish version prevails.*

# COOKIES POLICY

**Vesteri website and platform** · Version 1.2 · Effective from: 22 October 2026

## 1. What cookies are and who uses them

Cookies and similar technologies (localStorage, pixels, tags) are used by Time2Show, Inc. (Vesteri brand) and — with your consent — our partners. Legal basis: Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 and the GDPR.

## 2. The vesteri.com website

The website uses **strictly necessary cookies only**: it remembers the language you select (`NEXT_LOCALE`, up to 12 months) and relies on the infrastructure cookies our hosting provider sets for security and bot protection. Both are needed to deliver the service you requested and therefore require no consent.

We measure visits with **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which stores no cookies and no identifiers on your device.

Because the website sets no cookies that require consent, it displays no consent banner and offers no cookie preference panel.

## 3. The Vesteri webinar platform

The platform at vesteri.estalara.com (marketplace, accounts, LIVE presentations) has its own **[Cookies Policy](https://vesteri.estalara.com/en/legal/cookie)** and its own consent banner. If technologies requiring consent are ever introduced on vesteri.com, we will implement the same mechanism and update this Policy in advance.

## 4. Recipients

Infrastructure and analytics providers serving the website (currently Cloudflare). Once advertising campaigns and the platform go live, also Meta Platforms Ireland, Google Ireland/Google LLC, TikTok Technology Ltd. and LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Co. — to the extent described in the Privacy Policy (sections 7–8).
